SALON POLICIES

1. Appointment Policy

- We recommend booking appointments in advance to ensure the best service experience.
- Please arrive on time. Clients arriving more than 10 minutes late may be asked to reschedule or have their service adjusted, depending on the remaining available time.
- Walk-in clients are welcome based on salon availability.

2. Deposit Policy

- A deposit of $50 or 50% of the service total may be required for:
  - Services totaling over $100.
  - Services that require extended appointment time.
  - Group appointments.
- Deposits will be applied toward the final service balance.
- Deposits are non-refundable but may be transferred to another appointment if at least 24 hours’ notice is provided.

3. Cancellation & No-Show Policy

- Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice for any cancellations or appointment changes.
- Last-minute cancellations or no-shows without notice may result in the loss of your deposit.
- Repeated no-shows may require full prepayment for future appointments.

4. Group Booking Policy

- For groups of 3 or more guests, please book in advance to allow proper scheduling of staff and service time.
- A $50 deposit or 50% of the total service cost may be required for group appointments.
- The person making the reservation is responsible for providing each guest’s service details so we can prepare accordingly.
- Please provide at least 48 hours’ notice for changes or cancellations to group appointments.
- Reducing the number of guests or canceling at the last minute may affect the deposit.
- All group members are expected to arrive on time. Late arrivals may result in service adjustments based on salon availability.

5. Payment Policy

- Payment is due immediately after completion of services.
- We accept the following payment methods:
  - Cash
  - Credit/Debit Cards
  - Apple Pay
  - Zelle
  - Cash App

6. Service Guarantee Policy

- If you experience any service-related issues, such as lifting, chipping, or workmanship concerns, please contact us within 7 days of your appointment.
- Complimentary repairs are provided for workmanship-related issues only.
- Repairs do not apply to damage caused by accidents, improper use, or other factors beyond our control.
- No refunds will be issued for completed services.

7. Pricing Policy

- Service prices may vary depending on nail length, shape, design complexity, and additional requests.
- Additional charges may apply for intricate nail art, extra length, nail repairs, removal of gel or acrylic applied by another salon, or other special requests.
- Whenever possible, any additional charges will be discussed before the service begins.

8. Nail Art (Design) Policy

- Our online booking system lists Nail Art (Design) as a $50 / 1-hour service. This is a placeholder to let us know that you would like nail art.
- The $50 price and 1-hour appointment are not fixed. The final price and service time will vary depending on the complexity of the design.
- Nail art must be booked together with a base service (such as Gel Manicure, Builder Gel, Gel-X, Dip, Acrylic, etc.). We do not provide nail art on nails done by another salon without performing a new base service first.
- If you select the Nail Art (Design) option when booking, please text us at (857) 999-8268 with a photo or description of your desired design. We will review your request, provide an estimated price, and adjust your appointment time if needed.

9. Personal Belongings Policy

- Please keep your personal belongings with you during your appointment.
- The salon is not responsible for any lost, stolen, or damaged personal belongings.

10. Respect Policy

- We strive to provide a welcoming, comfortable, and respectful environment for all clients and staff.
- Please treat our team and other guests with kindness and respect.
- We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone displaying abusive, threatening, inappropriate, or disrespectful behavior toward our staff or other guests.

Thank You

Thank you for choosing our salon. We truly appreciate your trust and support. We look forward to welcoming you and providing you with the best possible service.
